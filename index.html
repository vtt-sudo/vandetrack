FAQ

Common questions, straight answers

Which tracker is right for my bike or car?

For personal anti-theft use, the S11 Mini (hidden magnetic) or a wired 2G/4G tracker works best. Message us your vehicle model on WhatsApp and we'll recommend the exact device — no over-selling.

Is AIS-140 really mandatory for my commercial vehicle?

Yes — public and commercial transport vehicles must carry an AIS-140 certified VLTD as per government norms. The VT-140 is fully compliant and our team handles installation + platform onboarding.

Do I need separate devices for CGM and VLTD?

No — the same AIS-140 certified VT-140 does both. For CGM (fleet/goods monitoring) it feeds your private dashboard; for the VLTD mandate it reports to the government VLT platform. One hardware, both jobs — no double cost.

Is there a monthly/yearly subscription?

Device price and platform subscription are quoted separately and clearly on WhatsApp before you pay. No hidden costs — that's a promise.

Do you install outside Gujarat too?

We support pan-India dispatch with installation guidance, and have a growing dealer network for on-site installs. Dealers in your area are shared on call.

What warranty do devices carry?

Every VandeTrack device comes with a manufacturer warranty (period varies by model — we quote it with the price, in writing, on WhatsApp). During warranty, repair/replacement is handled by our team — no running around.

How do I become a dealer or distributor?

Fill the form on the Dealership page or WhatsApp us "DEALER". We share margins, stock plans and onboarding steps on a call — most partners start within a week.
